Accountability
Rendering to each and all what is due to them (their moral and legal rights) in proportion to their agency. Equity (epieikeia) covers irregular cases so that the ordinance is framed to fit the circumstances.

Heteronomy
Acting under rules or influences imposed from outside yourself, rather than from your own reasoned will.
