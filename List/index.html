Ethics & Value Theory 5

Moral Agency & Responsibility

Accountability
Being answerable for your actions and their consequences. Audit your motives against an impartial standard, applying it to all parties in proportion to what is due to each, honoring its purpose over its letter.
Bad Faith
Lying to yourself that you had no freedom of choice in order to escape responsibility.
Heteronomy
Acting under rules or influences imposed from outside yourself, rather than from your own reasoned will.
