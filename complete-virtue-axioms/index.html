Module I

Integrity of Belief and Conduct

Axioms 1–7

Belief Evaluation

  1. 1

    Evidence is the sole legitimate ground of belief.

  2. 2

    An impartial standard binds the evaluator first, and binds the one who denies as much as the one who discloses. Denial carries the burden of proof, and no record is not a clean record.

Conduct Accountability

  1. 3

    Proportion is the measure of application: care follows need; presence and trust follow record.

  2. 4

    Motives are subject to the same scrutiny as actions.

  3. 5

    The aim of a standard takes precedence over its letter.

  4. 6

    Accountability is proportional to agency. Ignorance excuses only when inquiry wasn't neglected, and refused evidence compounds culpability. Blame follows intent; protection follows impact.

  5. 7

    Trust, in oneself first, follows a demonstrated pattern of conduct and is extended as the stakes allow. Candor about difficulty is evidence of integrity.

Module II

Emotional Regulation

Axioms 8–14

Interoceptive Decentering

  1. 8

    Sensation is physiological data; its proper place is the body, not the judgment.

  2. 9

    Between impulse and action there must be space in which the rational intellect can deliberate.

  3. 10

    Defensive compliance (fear-of-abandonment compulsion) remains compliance, whatever disguise it wears.

Acceptance and Recovery

  1. 11

    Interdependence is the human condition. Need is a claim on the whole circle of care, met in proportion to capacity, never at the cost of the giver's dignity or basic needs.

  2. 12

    Planned recovery and rest are components of performance, not interruptions.

Stress Responses

  1. 13

    The response must be proportional to the intensity of the stressor.

  2. 14

    Self-protection under acute pressure is a boundary, not an escape; under mild or moderate pressure, the stressor is reframed as manageable challenge.

Module III

Self-Concept

Axioms 15–21

Self-Worth

  1. 15

    Human dignity is grounded in the capacity for moral excellence; one honors it through disposition toward that excellence.

  2. 16

    Counterdependence is fear wearing the mask of strength.

  3. 17

    A setback is information about skill, not a verdict on worth.

Self-Confidence Loop

  1. 18

    Confidence is valid only in proportion to capability.

  2. 19

    Achievements from deliberate effort are the sole valid input to confidence.

  3. 20

    Confidence updates when evidence of capability changes.

  4. 21

    Increased confidence exists to be exercised on progressively harder tasks. Act before certainty when the risk is your own; grade and backstop it when it falls on someone vulnerable.

Module IV

Sustainable Action

Axioms 22–30

Situational Clarity

  1. 22

    Perception is representation; verify conditions, not just symptoms, before responding. Silence is not health, and disclosed distress is not unfitness.

  2. 23

    A fact survives semifactual ("even if") testing; a presupposition does not.

Strategic Focus

  1. 24

    Under overload, subtract before adding: basic needs first.

  2. 25

    Control is partial; the illusion of complete control must be renounced.

  3. 26

    Sunk costs are not reasons; only prospective value is.

Sustainable Perseverance

  1. 27

    Perseverance is tempered by courage and sustained by the mercy of self-acceptance, so it bends without breaking.

  2. 28

    Purpose (why) governs goals and methods (what, how); attachment to specific outcomes does not.

  3. 29

    Resilience is built through gradual, controlled exposure.

  4. 30

    Commitment belongs to long-term purpose and values; strategy may adapt.

Across all modules

Recurring Principles

  • Proportionality is the master relation.

  • Reality is the arbiter.

  • Fear presents as strength.

  • The boundary is the instrument of reason (space between stimulus and response).

  • Withholding truth from those who would weaponize it is discretion, not deception.

  • Allocation of risk.

    • Risk falls on those who create it.
    • Disclosure is not consent to penalty.
    • The vulnerable, oneself included, are staked only gradually and with backup.
