Named ideas

    Articles

      • Accountability ↗

        Rendering to each and all what is due to them (their moral and legal rights) in proportion to their agency. Equity (epieikeia) covers irregular cases.

      • Bad faith ↗

        Lying to yourself that you had no freedom of choice in order to escape responsibility.

      • Heteronomy ↗

        Acting under rules or influences imposed from outside yourself, rather than from your own reasoned will.

      • Instrumental and intrinsic value ↗

        The distinction between things valued as a means to something else and things valued for their own sake.

      • Value theory ↗

        The study of what is good or valuable, and why.

      • Coherence theory of truth ↗

        View that a belief is true if it fits consistently within a whole system of beliefs.

      • Gödel's completeness theorem ↗

        Theorem showing that in first-order logic, every logically valid statement can be formally proven.

      • Law of excluded middle ↗

        Logical principle that posits that every claim is either true or false, with no third option.

      • Argument from beauty ↗

        Argument for the existence of God.

      • Molinism ↗

        Theological position on God's middle knowledge.

      • Secondary causation ↗

        The natural world unfolds through its intrinsic potentialities and natural laws, with God as the first cause.

      • Teleology ↗

        Explaining things by their purpose, goal, or end.

      • Transcendentals ↗

        Properties of being shared by everything that exists, traditionally the true, the good, and the beautiful.

      • Social contract ↗

        Theory that political authority rests on an agreement among individuals to accept common rules.

      • State of nature ↗

        The hypothetical condition of human life before any government or society exists.

      • Might makes right ↗

        View that morality is, or ought to be, determined by power.

      • Rule of law ↗

        The principle that all people are subject to and accountable to the same laws, with no one being above them.

      • Broaden-and-build ↗

        Theory of positive emotions.

      • Eustress ↗

        Beneficial stress.

      • Counterdependency ↗

        Defensive refusal to rely on others, often masking a fear of closeness or dependence.

      • Grit ↗

        Perseverance and passion toward long-term goals.

      • Self-control ↗

        The ability to regulate impulses, emotions, and behavior in pursuit of longer-term goals.

      • Interoception ↗

        Sensing the internal state of your own body, such as heartbeat, hunger, or tension.

      • Polyvagal theory ↗

        Theory of how the autonomic nervous system shifts between states of safety, mobilization, and shutdown.

      • Behavioral economics ↗

        Study of how psychological factors and biases shape people's economic decisions.
