      Belief Evaluation

      • Test each premise against the intellectual golden rule (an impartial standard applied proportionally to all), ensuring that there is alignment between each inference and the empirical evidence.

      Conduct Accountability

      • Render to each and all what is due to them (their moral and legal rights) in proportion to their functional agency. Equity (epieikeia) covers irregular cases so that the ordinance is framed to fit the circumstances.

      Interoceptive Decentering

      • Observe your experiences as they unfold without reacting to them automatically. Psychologically distance yourself from your thoughts and emotions, recognizing how your underlying assumptions and immediate emotional reactions are shaping your appraisal of the situation.
      • Delay your automatic, habitual, and learned reactions to stimuli, in order to select a more appropriate behavior that is consistent with completing your goals.
      • Recognize defensive compulsions (caused by fear-of-abandonment) as submissive compliance in disguise.

      Acceptance and Recovery

      • Let go of the pretense of invulnerability to recognize that our interdependence demands mutual cooperation
      • Treat planned recovery and rest as essential maintenance needed for sustained performance. In this way the exhaustion stage does not reduce the gains achieved, the body can recover and remain above the original equilibrium point.

      Stress Triage

      • Under acute or overwhelming pressure, set healthy boundaries and disengage. Protecting your personal values from being compromised or violated.
      • Under mild or moderate pressure: push your boundaries through controlled exposure techniques to reframe risk as a manageable challenge. The goal should not be too far out of reach but still be slightly more than one can handle. This fosters challenge and motivation since the goal is in sight.

      Self-Worth

      • Your human dignity rests on your capacity for moral excellence; and your disposition toward it is how you honor it.
      • Recognize that defensive self-sufficiency is counterdependence (the refusal of attachment, personal need and dependency out of fear of vulnerability).
      • Develop a growth mindset by viewing setbacks as skill-deficit feedback rather than as personal flaws. Failures don't constitute grounds for self-condemnation.

      Self-Confidence Loop

      1. A Internalize achievements (stemming from your deliberate effort) to cultivate self-belief and strengthen your perseverance.
      2. B Proportion confidence to your actual level of capability.
      3. C Update confidence when new evidence indicates a change in your capabilities.
      4. D Use your confidence (grounded in the accurate appraisal of your capabilities) to take on progressively more challenging tasks.

      Simulation Heuristics

      • Use "even if" simulations (considering another outcome based on a different path, and the possibility of taking that path in the future to avoid undesired outcomes) to distinguish objective facts from false presuppositions.

      Strategic Focus

      • During cognitive overload, set aside nonessential tasks to refocus on basic needs and prevent future burnout. Recovery from exhaustion is found in what is undemanding and joyful. Sleep and physical exercise are the basis of recovery and should be prioritized initially.
      • The illusion of control is the tendency for people to overestimate their ability to control events. Avoid this illusion and recognize that some situations are contingent on external forces and are inherently unpredictable.
      • Don't become attached to sunk costs or let them influence future decisions. Sunk costs are irrelevant to rational decisions.

      Sustainable Perseverance

      • Perseverance is sustained by the mercy of self-compassion (extending compassion to one's self in instances of perceived inadequacy, failure, or general suffering).
      • People are inspired by a sense of purpose. Let your underlying purpose (the why) shape your goals and methods (what and how), allowing you to become detached from specific outcomes.
      • Build resilience through gradual, controlled exposure to stressors. Increasing the intensity after a successful demonstration of proficiency. Exposed to sufficient stress to develop coping skills, but not so much that it overwhelms you.

      Committed Action

      • Set goals according to your values and carry them out responsibly, in the service of a meaningful life. Creative hopelessness is a technique used to give up ineffective approaches and facilitate acceptance.
